How it works

From a folder of PDFs to a decision you can defend.

01 / 04

Ingest everything

Drop in contracts, purchase orders, statements of work, tracking spreadsheets and meeting transcripts. PDFs, Excel, CSV and text are read in full in your browser — nothing leaves your machine until you run an analysis.

  • Record details are extracted from what you ingest, not typed in by hand
  • Teams and Zoom transcripts in .vtt, .srt, .txt and .docx
  • Names and contact details in a transcript are replaced before it is stored
02 / 04

Extract with confidence

Annual value, term dates, notice periods, uplift schedules, liability caps, auto-renewal traps. Each one is scored for confidence and shown with the line of the contract it came from, so you can check it rather than take it on trust.

  • Cost, users, risk, opportunities, compliance and obligations, each on its own tab
  • Anything below 60% confidence is flagged for a human to look at
  • An empty field means "not found", never "not present"
03 / 04

Ask Cedric anything

A conversational analyst that answers only from the documents you have ingested and the analysis run over them. When does this renew, what is missing, where is there room to negotiate — named to a contract, so the answer can be traced back to a source.

  • Scoped to one contract at a time, by reference, name or supplier
  • No general knowledge and no guessing beyond what you have given it
  • Two credits a question, metered the same way as everything else
04 / 04

Export for the boardroom

Negotiation levers, recommendations and the dates you cannot afford to miss, written as something you can put in front of a stakeholder. Out as Word, as a PDF, or as an outline to build a deck from.

  • Renewal alerts export as calendar files that fire from your own diary
  • Every report carries the audit trail behind the figures
  • A screening aid for a commercial decision — not legal advice
